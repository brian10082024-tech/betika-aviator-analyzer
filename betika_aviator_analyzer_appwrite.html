What this prototype can and cannot do

It can read uploaded screenshots, save multipliers and calculate sample frequencies for 1.5x, 2x, 3x and 5x.

It does not yet monitor Betika live or capture another app's screen automatically. That requires a native Android app and explicit Android screen-capture permission.
